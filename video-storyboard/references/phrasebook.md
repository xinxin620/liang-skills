# 英文标准词表

只从这几个表里取词。同义替换会让模型对同一动作产生不同理解。

## 景别

| 景别 | 英文 | 用途 |
|---|---|---|
| 远景 | Wide shot | 交代环境、走位 |
| 全景 | Full shot | 全身＋站位 |
| 中景 | Medium shot | 单人动作、说话 |
| 中近景 | Medium close-up | 说话的特写替代 |
| 双人中景 | Medium two-shot | 两人同框（最常用来卡嘴型） |
| 近景 | Close shot | 手部以上，情绪 |
| 特写 | Close-up | 脸、手的局部 |
| 大特写 | Extreme close-up | 道具、指尖、眼神 |
| 过肩 | Over-the-shoulder | 建立关系与视线 |

## 镜头覆盖（单人镜 / 双人镜）

| 中文 | 英文 |
|---|---|
| 单人镜（最佳，零串嘴风险） | Medium close shot, <角色> alone, sharp |
| 越肩，听者前景虚化 | over-the-shoulder, the listener a soft foreground blur |
| 双人中景 | Medium two-shot |
| 两人都实焦（仅无台词时用） | both faces sharp, no dialogue |
| 听者在画外 | the listener is out of frame |

## 情绪配可见动作（情绪副词 + 身体小动作）

情绪副词放前面，可见动作放后面，两句都给，表演才落地。

| 情绪（英文副词） | 配套的可见动作 |
|---|---|
| quiet and halting, each word costing her something | one hand tightens on her sleeve |
| holding back, deliberately flat | her jaw sets, eyes on the ground |
| warm but careful | a half-smile that does not reach his eyes |
| brimming but not yet spilling | her lips press together and tremble |
| composed, a trace of warmth | she settles her hands in her sleeves |
| quiet, humbled | her head stays bowed, shoulders trembling |
| brooks no refusal | her tone flat, palm open, arm steady |

## 固定句尾

- 角色声明收尾：`Preserve the facial features, costume, body proportions, and overall appearance from the reference image.`
- 场景段收尾：`Preserve its layout, palette and lighting exactly.`
- 站位锁定：`keep the same screen arrangement - do not mirror it.`
- 声音锁定收尾：`Do not change.`
- 嘴型纪律：`only the character who is speaking moves their lips. Every other character - including anyone sharing the frame - keeps their mouth closed and completely still.`
- 硬切：`hard cuts only between shots.`

## 接戏（边界类型决定用哪组）

### 紧接：承接三件套

| 中文 | 英文 |
|---|---|
| 延续同一个半步，尚未站定 | continuing the same half-step, not yet complete |
| 她的手指还停在袖口上，没有收回去 | her hand is still resting on the sleeve, not yet withdrawn |
| 动作停在中途 | the movement stops mid-way |
| 不许把动作重做一遍 | no restarting the movement |
| 不许从这个动作的开头重放 | no re-playing the step from the beginning |
| 承接上一镜的姿势，不重置 | picking up from the previous pose, no reset |

### 跳切 / 省略：主动断

| 中文 | 英文 |
|---|---|
| 不与上一镜对视线 | no matching eyeline to the previous shot |
| 不重演上一镜的姿势 | no re-playing the previous pose |
| 不许有动作跨剪切延续 | no continuous action carrying across the cut |
| 时间已跳过 | time has moved on |
| 直接切到新状态 | cut straight to the new state |
