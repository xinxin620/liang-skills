# 已定稿样例（唯一风格基准）

写新镜头时，先读这两条，再动笔。句式、语气、详略程度以此为准。

## 幕五「不许解」（第三场 其五）—— 单人镜覆盖法范例

15 秒 / 4 镜 / 3 句台词。**这份是目前质量最高的一条：每句台词都由"单人实焦"镜头承担，串嘴风险为零。**

```
幕五「不许解」（第三场 其五）
建议时长 15 秒。参考图：<Picture 1> 十一娘、<Picture 2> 青儿、<Picture 7> 封三娘、<Picture 10> 幕四成品帧（站位与场景基准）。
<Subject 1> is Shiniang in <Picture 1>: a young noblewoman, black hair in a large soft high bun pinned with small gold floral hairpins and wispy loose strands at her temples and nape, wearing a sheer pale-pink patterned gauze robe over a white high-waisted dress with a pale sash. Preserve the facial features, hairstyle, costume, body proportions, and overall appearance from the reference image.
<Subject 2> is Qing'er in <Picture 2>: a young maid with a round face and two long dark braids, wearing a teal cross-collar top with white trim, a dark green waist sash, and a light khaki-green pleated skirt. Preserve the facial features, hairstyle, costume, body proportions, and overall appearance from the reference image.
<Subject 3> is Feng in <Picture 7>: a young woman with long black hair styled half-up and half-down, loose waves over her shoulders, the upper sections tied with pale pink ribbons at the back, small gold floral hairpins set with tiny pearls at her temples, and a small beauty mark below the left corner of her lips, wearing a pale pink cross-collared robe with a white inner layer and a pink waist sash. Preserve the facial features, hairstyle, costume, body proportions, and overall appearance from the reference image.
<Picture 10> is the previous shot's frame: the exact courtyard, lighting, prop arrangement and character positions to continue from.

<Subject 1>, S1 is always the speaker. S1 always uses one fixed voice: a clear, cool young woman's voice, soft in volume - now low and earnest. Do not change.
<Subject 3>, S3 is always the speaker. S3 always uses one fixed voice: a clear, lively young woman's voice - now quiet, humbled. Do not change.

Scene: continue directly from the frame in <Picture 10> - reproduce this arrangement exactly, do not mirror it, do not rearrange it. The same autumn inner courtyard in late afternoon golden light: grey-brick east wall with grey tile coping along the right, beds of yellow and white chrysanthemums at its base, grey stone paving scattered with dry golden leaves, the large golden-leaf tree on the left shading the round stone table and stools, the low wooden tea table with the celadon teapot and small cups in the right foreground. Realistic period-drama film look, warm slanting sunlight, muted palette, shallow depth of field.

Heights stay fixed in every shot: Shiniang is tallest; the top of Feng's head reaches about Shiniang's brow level; Qing'er is the shortest, the top of her head reaching only about Shiniang's nose level.

Spatial axis (180-degree rule): Shiniang stands in the middle-left of the screen, body facing screen-right. Feng stands facing her on the right, body facing screen-left, by the low wooden tea table. Qing'er stands far in the background on the left by the stone table under the tree, holding her bamboo broom, watching from a distance, soft focus. The camera stays on the courtyard side of the Shiniang-Feng line for the entire scene; no crossing to the other side, no swapping left and right.

[Shot 1] Medium close shot, Feng alone, sharp, standing on the right: her eyes stay lowered, voice quiet and halting, each word costing her something; one hand tightens on her sleeve as she speaks. S3 says:
<d>[Chinese]我娘其实在很远的地方。当日说近村，说的是舅家。姊妹，您是人家的女儿，同是女娇，哪还没进门槛，先自矮了一截似的浑身寒碜着。不敢来。</d>
Mouth discipline: only Feng moves her lips; no other character shares the frame.
[Shot 2] Two-shot, no dialogue: Shiniang steps forward and takes both of Feng's hands in hers, pulling her a half-step closer; Feng's head stays bowed, her shoulders trembling. Tight framing on the joined hands and the two faces. Mouths closed, both still.
[Shot 3] Medium close shot, Shiniang alone, sharp: she holds Feng's gaze, speaking slowly, each word a promise. S1 says:
<d>[Chinese]我眼里从来没有贵贱。你既认了我这个姊妹，往后不许解。</d>
Mouth discipline: only Shiniang moves her lips; no other character shares the frame.
[Shot 4] Close shot, Feng alone, sharp: her eyes lift, brimming but not yet spilling; her lips press together and tremble, holding back tears she is losing the fight against. No dialogue, mouth closed.

Forbidden: no subtitles, no on-screen text, no watermark; no fades, no dissolves, no push transitions; no left-right swaps, no face or hand fusion; no simultaneous mouth movement - every dialogue line is covered by a shot in which only ONE face is in sharp focus, the listener is either out of frame or a soft foreground blur.
Mandatory: no BGM; ambient + voices only (autumn wind, distant insects, faint leaves); hard cuts only between shots.
```

**这一份示范了六件事，写新镜头时照做：**

1. **单人镜覆盖法** —— 3 句台词全是 `<角色> alone, sharp`，每镜跟一句 `Mouth discipline: only X moves her lips; no other character shares the frame.` 青儿虽在数值上是 Subject 2，本场无台词，所以**完全不写她的音色**。
2. **成品帧续拍** —— `<Picture 10>` 一句顶三张图（场景＋站位＋道具＋光线），配 `do not mirror it, do not rearrange it`。
3. **声音锁两截** —— 破折号前是终身音色，`- now quiet, humbled` 是本场状态。
4. **站位写身体朝向** —— `stands in the middle-left of the screen, body facing screen-right`，不只写左右。
5. **情绪配可见动作** —— `each word costing her something; one hand tightens on her sleeve`。
6. **待机角色给动作** —— 青儿 `holding her bamboo broom, watching from a distance, soft focus`，否则会被模型删掉。

**一处可挑的地方**：Shot 2 是双人镜，写了 `Tight framing on the joined hands and the two faces`（两张脸都实焦）。这一镜无台词，所以不违规；但如果后面要把台词挪进来，必须改成过肩＋听者前景虚化。

## 汉服石阶对峙 —— 不可能动作（瞬移）范例 ⭐

5 镜。**这份的价值全在"她瞬间消失"怎么处理：不拍位移过程。** 是目前唯一一条覆盖特效 / 不可能动作的样例。

```
汉服石阶对峙

按图三把石阶、牌坊、黄昏雾的光影和站位全部固定下来，一共 5 个镜头。有一处关键处理：她瞬间消失的那一段不拍位移过程，否则模型一定会渲染成慢速飘移。改成"原地闪一下 + 空气炸开 + 空出的台阶"，再直接切到她已经在空地上站好。

| 镜头 | 景别 | 内容 |
| 1 | 中景侧拍（半身） | 她在左、老者右半身在焦外，说完那句话，头不动 |
| 2 | 固定大全 | 台阶空了，白光一闪，冲击波把雾和灰掀开，衣服和头发的残影散掉，一声脆响 |
| 3 | 硬切 | 她已经站在台阶下的平地上，衣袖还在落，雾绕回脚边 |
| 4 | 老者中景 | 杖离地又落下，缓慢逼近，衣摆拖过湿石 |
| 5 | 双人全景 | 她左侧、他右侧（高她一头），杖斜挑，双脚站定，中间是空台阶和雾，定住 |

她换到平地后仍在画面左侧，这样轴线就没断，后面接着打不会翻边。
```

```text
<Subject 1> is the Girl in <Picture 1>: a young woman in layered grey-blue and ivory Chinese hanfu - high hair bun with gold and pearl hairpins, loose strands at the temples, wide broad sleeves. Preserve her facial features, hairstyle, costume and body proportions from <Picture 1>. <Picture 3> shows how she is lit and dressed in this scene - keep that exactly.

<Subject 2> is the Elder in <Picture 2>: an elderly man in a dark charcoal hanfu robe with grey crane embroidery over a black inner robe, silver-white hair in a high topknot with a wooden hairpin, a long white beard to the chest, a deep-lined face, a gnarled wooden staff in his right hand. Preserve his facial features, beard, hairstyle, costume and body proportions from <Picture 2>. <Picture 3> shows how he is lit and dressed in this scene - keep that exactly.

<Picture 3> is the master frame for this scene: this exact stone stairway, the ruined gate arch at upper left with dusk sky burning through it, the flat stone courtyard at the base of the steps, dark bamboo and mountain ridges behind, low fog across the ground, cold grey-blue grade with one band of dying amber light. Reproduce this location and this blocking exactly - do not mirror it, do not rearrange it, do not change the time of day.

<Subject 1>, S1 is always the speaker: a low, level young female voice, dry and hard, no tremor. Do not change.
<Subject 2>, S2 stays silent in this scene and does not speak at all.

Spatial axis (180-degree rule): The Girl is stage-left, her body three-quarters toward frame right, facing the Elder. The Elder is stage-right, his body three-quarters toward frame left, facing her. The camera stays on the same side of their axis for the whole scene: never crosses the line, never swaps their screen left-right positions, never reverses their facing or eye-line direction. Every shot keeps her on the left and him on the right.

Heights stay fixed in every shot: on level ground the Elder stands a full head taller than the Girl with a straighter back; on the stairway she is higher because she stands steps above him. When they both stand on the flat courtyard, he is again a full head taller.

Scene: late dusk, heavy cold fog lying flat across the stone. Wind moves the fog and the loose fabric. No warm light on the characters - only the cold ambient and the distant amber band behind the gate.

[Shot 1] Medium two-shot, side on, both from the waist up, camera on the same side of the axis.
<Subject 1> is sharp in the left half of frame, three-quarter profile, chin slightly down, facing frame right.

[Shot 2] Locked-off wide of the stairway, no faces in frame. The step where she stood is now empty. A hard white-green flash, one frame, then a ring of air bursts outward from that spot and blows the fog and the loose dust off the stone; a shred of fabric and hair blur is tearing apart in place and gone. The step is bare. One sharp cracking sound, like a whip close to the ear.

[Shot 3] Hard cut. She is already standing on the flat stone courtyard at the foot of the stairs, stage-left, side-on profile to camera, facing frame right. Her robe is still settling, the loose strands of hair fall back into place, dust rolls away from her feet and the fog folds back around her legs. She is still. No travel, no run, no arc - she was not there, now she is.

[Shot 4] Cut to <Subject 2>. Medium shot, stage-right, sharp. The staff lifts off the stone and comes back down with weight; he walks forward at a slow, heavy, unhurried pace, hem dragging across the wet stone, the fog parting at his boots. He does not hurry. His eyes stay on her.

[Shot 5] Wider two-shot from the same side of the axis: <Subject 1> stands stage-left on the flat courtyard facing frame right, sleeves settling, chin up. <Subject 2> stops stage-right facing frame left, a full head taller, staff angled slightly out from his body, feet planted shoulder width. Empty wet stone and drifting fog between them. Both motionless, both ready. Hold two beats, cut.

Mouth discipline: only the character who is speaking moves their lips. Every other character - including anyone sharing the frame - keeps their mouth closed and completely still. No simultaneous mouth movement in any shot, ever. Only the speaker's face is sharp and only their lips move; the listener is a soft blur and their mouth does not move at all.

Forbidden: no subtitles, no on-screen text, no watermark, no lower-thirds; no soft dissolves, fades, wipes, zoom or dolly push transitions - hard cuts only; no crossing the 180-degree axis, no left-right swap, no mirroring; no visible travel path, no running, no jump, no leap, no flying, no arcing trajectory for the girl; no glowing trail, no light beam, no magic particles, no energy aura, no CG smoke, no sparkling dust; no slow motion, no speed ramp, no freeze frame; no face or hand fusion, no extra fingers; no costume, hairpin or staff change between shots; no modern objects; no glossy beauty-retouch skin, no plastic sheen; no BGM, no mood music; no fantasy glow.

Mandatory: hard cuts only between shots; ambient sound and voices only, no BGM; ambience is wind through bamboo, wind over stone, staff on wet stone, silk and fabric; the displacement is a violent physical snap, not magic; period-drama realism, cold and restrained; the girl's feet never leave the ground except in Shot 2's flash.
```

**这份示范了七件事：**

1. **三拍结构处理瞬移**（Shot 1 前一态 → Shot 2 无脸的闪光帧 → Shot 3 硬切到后一态＋余韵）。`she was not there, now she is` 是全篇最有用的一句。
2. **位移后保持原屏幕侧** —— 她从台阶移到平地，仍在 stage-left，轴线不断。
3. **身高分情况写** —— 台阶上她高、平地上他一头，一句讲清两种落点，否则模型会在台阶上把两人拍成一样高。
4. **沉默要声明** —— `<Subject 2>, S2 stays silent in this scene and does not speak at all.` 比省略强。
5. **Forbidden 三层齐** —— 通用层（字幕/转场/越轴/脸手）＋特效层（路径、慢动作、特效化三类）＋AI 感层（磨皮、塑料反光、奇幻光晕）。
6. **唯一例外收紧口子** —— `the girl's feet never leave the ground except in Shot 2's flash.`
7. **交付五件齐** —— 标题 → 关键处理说明 → 分镜表 → 英文 prompt → 中文对照。

**⚠️ 这份有一个必须补的缺陷：台词丢了。**

分镜表第 1 镜写着"说完那句话"，但英文 prompt 里**一个 `<d>` 标签都没有**——"多说无益，今天说什么你也要死在这里"这句台词完全没落地。Shot 1 还缺本镜的 `Mouth discipline: only … moves her lips` 行。

**改写 Shot 1 时应为：**

```
[Shot 1] Medium two-shot, side on, both from the waist up, camera on the same side of the axis. <Subject 1> is sharp in the left half of frame, three-quarter profile, chin slightly down, facing frame right; the Elder is a soft out-of-focus mass at the right edge, his face not readable. She holds her chin still as she speaks. S1 says:
<d>[Chinese]多说无益。今日无论你说什么，你都要死在这里。</d>
Mouth discipline: only the Girl moves her lips; the Elder's face is out of focus and his mouth does not move.
```

## 青石广场缠斗 —— 高动态模式范例 ⭐

12 拍 / 无胜负 / 三段式（起势 3 拍 → 交锋 6 拍 → 收势 3 拍）。**这是唯一一条武戏样例，结构规则见 `references/mode-action.md`。**

> ⚠️ 原件把 `overall_soundscape` 与 `non_diegetic_music` 写成了中文。H3 这两个是官方英文字段，**应写英文**（下面保留原样以存真，改写时按 mode-action.md 的英文规范走）。

```
角色锁定区：
<Subject 1>
年轻女子，面容、高髻与金珠发簪、灰蓝米白双层汉服、宽袖、身形以参考图为准，全程锁定。
<Subject 2>
白发老者，面容、长银须、木簪发髻、深灰鹤纹外袍与黑色内袍、粗木杖、身形以参考图为准，全程锁定。
<Subject 3>

**v2 → v3 的四处推进：**

1. **状态锚点取代成品帧** —— 上一镜**还没出片**，手上只有一张构图接近的参考图。于是不套成品帧，改用纯文字写 `<Subject 3> 当前对局状态参考`：双方相对位置 → 武器各自在哪 → 环境色调 → 屏幕左右 → 轴线声明。**这是续拍最轻的写法**，代价是精确度低于成品帧（锁了关系，没锁光线的实际方向与道具的实际摆位）。
2. **镜头绑在武器上** —— `剑尖视点(Blade Cam)`，镜头绑着剑尖向前冲。比"低机位跟拍"更狠：观众不再看人挥剑，而是**跟着剑尖撞过去**。
3. **物件被打飞要甩出去再甩回来** —— 金珠发簪被打飞、`叮的一声`弹进雾里，镜头追着发簪甩过去、再急速甩回她脸上。镜头因此有了**人物之外的第二个动作主体**，画面不空。
4. **接触面分离声明** —— `剑刃与木杖接触面清晰分离、不融合，两人身体各自独立、不共用肢体`。v1 v2 只写身体分离，v3 把最容易糊的**武器相抵那一瞬**也钉住了。

**节奏上的差别：一拍之内双方各出手一次。**

v1 v2 是"一拍一人"。v3 更紧凑：她攻（撩剑划开外袍）→ 他聚力（双手改握杖中段、肩腰一沉）→ 他攻（大开大合横扫）→ 她防（仰身让杖）。**攻—聚力—反击—防**四步走完，观众才看到"交换"，而不是"轮流表演"。

**v3 自身待改：**

- **标签混用**：表头写 `<Subject 3>`，正文写 `<锚点4>`。同一份里两套写法，必须统一成 `<Subject N>`（本 skill 已定：两种模式共用 Subject）。
- **两处镜头词可疑**：`追臀用镜(Whip Pan)` 疑为「追簪用镜」误写（该拍正是追发簪）；`急速升镜(Tilt Up)` 与括注「升镜」语义重复。
- **连续拍容易退化成同机位连拍**：第二剑第三剑连刺、第一记第二记横扫，写完要逐拍核对高度与焦段，守住"每拍换机位"。
- **标称 12 拍，实际 13 处**标记。
- **无胜负的收尾在递减**：v1 有「谁都没有倒下」，v2 只留「重新锁定」，v3 改成「她退而不乱」。**本镜是有意改的**（追击段的收束，不是全场终局），但**全场最后一镜必须把无胜负明写回来**。

```

**这份示范了六件事：**

1. **量化到毫米与度数** —— 下颌角 `约 102 度`、头部微侧 `15 度`、光位 `45 度`、疤 `1.5cm`、不对称 `约 1mm`、斑 `1mm`。"脸型偏方"模型各解各的，`下颌角约 102 度` 只有一个解。
2. **识别特征是锁脸的真正抓手** —— 女版：右下颌角不对称 1mm + 左颧骨 1mm 浅斑；男版：左下颌 1.5cm 浅疤 + 右眉上方竖细直疤。**成对出现**（瑕疵 + 不对称 = 两个交叉验证点），换角度时至少一个可见。
3. **左上格蒙脸是刻意为之** —— 四视图管服装身形，脸在这里是干扰项。不蒙脸，模型会在四格里各生成一张脸，四张必然不一致（负面里那条"换脸"就是这么来的）。关键是 `保留头部外轮廓，不要裁掉头顶和下巴`，否则整体比例失真。
4. **服装写到内层** —— `立领内侧一线极窄冷青色灯带`、`左胸内侧露出半截青白玉数据链`、`下摆两侧开杈，露出内层石墨灰高领针织`。内层不写，换角度时内外层接不上。
5. **肤色与质感点名反 AI** —— `保留皮肤纹理与毛孔，不磨皮，冷白肤质`，负面再补 `皮褶、油光、塑料感、网红脸、滤镜`。
6. **总纲一句钉死四格一致性** —— `四格必须是同一个人、同一张脸、同一套衣服、同一光线，禁止格间换脸`。这一句不能省。
